How to Operate Your Modernfold Walls

Clear, practical guidance for facility staff and new team members. Watch the demos, follow the steps, and run your operable walls with confidence.

Acousti-Clear Glass Micro-Perf Partitions

Acousti-Clear Glass Walls

Automatic seals. No tools required. Smooth track turns and safe panel movement.

Live Demo Glass

Setup, operation & breakdown

  1. No crank handle needed — all seals are automatic on these glass walls.
  2. Align the male actuator (nose) with the female receiver on the jamb or previous panel.
  3. Give a firm, controlled push — a small amount of force activates the cam that deploys top and bottom seals.
  4. At 90° track turns, feel for the “dip” when the trolley hits the diverter; a little extra force helps the panel complete the turn.
  5. Keep panels aligned as you stack. Small misalignment early becomes a larger gap later.
  6. When breaking down, push the leading edge toward the room (mirror setups are opposite but follow the same logic).
  7. Slight sway with seals retracted is normal — the system is designed for that. Panels will not fall out of the track under normal use.
Pro tip: You don’t need to treat the glass like it’s fragile during normal operation. Move with confidence — the system is built for daily use.

Micro-Perf Operable Partitions

Acousti-Seal Encore Micro-Perf — blocks sound (STC) and absorbs it (NRC) for high-performance spaces.

Live Demo Solid / Micro-Perf

Setup, operation & breakdown

  1. Open the pocket doors fully before moving panels.
  2. Reach in and grab the groove on the second panel (not just the first) so the stack unfolds cleanly.
  3. Align the operable nose, then drop the bottom seal. Add a small extra turn for rigidity (seals can exert significant pressure).
  4. Extend the expandable nose almost fully, then finish the last bit of seal engagement.
  5. To break down: raise the bottom seal first, then retract the expandable nose. Feel for the definitive stop — don’t force past it.
  6. Fold paired panels at the hinge and return them to the pocket. Keep fingers clear of hinge points.
  7. Light friction/squeal from constant-contact top seals against the track is normal and expected.
Common issue: If panels look uneven after setup, one bottom seal may have caught the other. Raise the seal, realign, and re-drop. Easy fix.

Key things to remember

Quick reference for anyone operating the walls.

Alignment first

Small misalignment at the start compounds. Line panels up before engaging seals.

Seals have a stop

You’ll feel a clear stopping point when raising/retracting seals. Don’t force past it.

Glass can move with confidence

Acousti-Clear panels are tougher than they look. Normal operation does not require extreme caution.

Sway is normal

With seals retracted, panels may sway slightly. That’s by design — they stay in the track.
